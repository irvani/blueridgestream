## I. The Blue Ridge Stream B2B Referral Program Bylaws

To leverage the professional networks of lawyers, agricultural land bankers, forestry managers, and engineers, Blue Ridge Stream Restoration & Mitigation LLC operates a structured, three-track B2B referral reward program. This program maximizes high-converting lead acquisitions (such as degraded mountain trout wades, coastal marshlands, and industrial stream properties) by aligning direct cash or donation rewards with regional outdoor lifestyle incentives:

### Track A: Cash / Credit Referral Affiliate Fees
- **The Reward Structure**: For any licensed attorney, real estate broker, or relationship banker who refers a qualified landowner stream bank project that closes a 70/30 Joint-Venture contract, Blue Ridge pays an **Affiliate Finder's Fee** of **2.0%** of the gross stream mitigation credit sales allocated to the developer's share.
- **The Math**: On a standard 1,500 linear foot project (such as Anderson Creek) yielding 12,900 stream credits: sold at $110/credit, grossing $1,419,000. Under the 70/30 split, the developer collects 70% ($993,300). A 2.0% finder's fee yields a **19,866 USD cash payout** directly to the referring partner.
- **For Developer Referrals**: If a commercial developer refers another corporate developer in need of credits, they can opt to receive **equivalent physical stream credits** at a **25% wholesale discount** to satisfy their own upcoming USACE Nationwide Permit 27 obligations.

### Track B: Conservation Non-Profit Matching Allocations
- **The Reward Structure**: For partners who cannot accept direct cash compensation due to internal corporate policies, legal ethics rules, or government compliance (e.g., environmental attorneys, municipal engineers, utility planners), Blue Ridge offers a **Conservation Matching Donation**.
- **The Allocation**: Blue Ridge allocates **2.5% of the gross credit sales proceeds** ($24,832 USD on the Anderson Creek model) to a certified 501(c)(3) conservation non-profit of the partner's choice in their name. Supported charities include:
  - *Trout Unlimited (Georgia Chapter)*: Protecting coldwater mountain spawning gravels.
  - *The Nature Conservancy (Georgia Chapter)*: Riparian corridor acquisitions.
  - *Coastal Georgia Land Trust*: Estuarine and Sapelo Island marsh protection.
  - *Georgia Wildlife Federation*: Statewide game and fish habitat restoration.

### Track C: Monthly Private Mountain Fly-Fishing Sweepstakes
- **The Sweepstakes Structure**: Any partner who submits a qualified 'Stream Walk Lead' (a Georgia landowner with $\ge 50$ acres and $\ge 1,000$ LF of degraded stream buffer) is automatically entered into our **Monthly Mountain Fly-Fishing Sweepstakes**.
- **The Prize Experience**: Once a month, Blue Ridge draws one referring partner to win an all-inclusive, ultra-premium fly-fishing weekend for two:
  - *Private Water Wades*: Guided fly-fishing on the exclusive, private trophy-trout reaches of the Soque River or Toccoa River.
  - *Expert Drift Boat Guided Float*: Full-day float drift-boat fly-casting targeting wild browns and rainbows.
  - *Premium Lodging*: 2 nights of premium lodging at Smithgall Woods State Park or a high-end Blue Ridge mountain cabin.
  - *Elite Outfitting*: Complete Sage fly-rods, Ross reels, and custom fly packages provided as a permanent gift (valued at $2,500 USD).

### Track D: Landowner Joint-Venture Peer Referrals & Premium Estate Upgrades
- **The Reward Structure**: Rural landowners are the ultimate partners for expanding our stream restoration footprint. To encourage peer-to-peer landowner referrals, Blue Ridge operates a **Landowner Loyalty Reward Track**.
- **The Option 1 (JV Split Booster)**: If an existing Joint-Venture landowner refers another Georgia landowner (with a qualified degraded stream reach) and Blue Ridge signs a Joint-Venture agreement, the referring landowner's own Joint-Venture split ratio is boosted by **2.0%** (from 30.0% to 32.0% of gross credit revenues on their remaining credit release tranches).
- **The Option 2 (Premium On-Site Civil Upgrades)**: Landowners can choose to exchange their cash split booster for direct, premium land improvement upgrades executed on-site by Blue Ridge's heavy excavation construction crews at **0 USD cost to the owner**:
  - *Custom Private Trout Spawning Stream*: Design and physical excavation of a custom 100-linear-foot wild trout spawning channel (using native sorted gravels and mature hemlock/Rhododendron maximum shade protection) to enhance their private fishery.
  - *Heavy-Duty Stream Crossing Upgrades*: Design and construction of a solid timber-arch or bioengineered rock vehicular stream crossing (valued at $15,000 USD) to replace washed-out crossings on their own property.
  - *Creek-side Cedar Fishing Pavilion*: Planning and construction of a premium cedar fishing dock or riverside bench pavilion to enjoy their restored watercourses.
