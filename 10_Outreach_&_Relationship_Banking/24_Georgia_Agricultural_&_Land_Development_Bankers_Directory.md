## I. The Relationship Banking Playbook

To systematically acquire new projects and bypass competitor bidding wars, Hunter Morris must approach these bankers using two structured strategic plays:

### Play A: The 70/30 Landowner Credit-Split Joint Venture
Targeted at agricultural, forestry, and special assets bankers holding notes on tracts with impaired stream systems or facing debt covenants. Hunter pitches the bank to introduce him to the landowner: Blue Ridge will restore the stream and secure mitigation credits at zero upfront cost to the owner, sharing 70 percent of credit sales proceeds with the developer and 30 percent with the landowner. The landowner receives a debt-clearing cash infusion, and the banker secures their loan with prime, liquid ecological assets.

### Play B: The Direct Developer Stream Mitigation Offset
Targeted at commercial real estate (CRE) and mountain subdivision lenders. Bankers financing major grading subdivisions or retail parks encounter high USACE Savannah District Section 404 stream buffer impacts. These impacts require purchasing stream mitigation credits, which standard credit banks sell at steep broker-inflated rates. Hunter offers these developers a direct Permittee-Responsible Mitigation (PRM) or direct-sale mitigation package at a 30 percent discount, saving them hundreds of thousands of dollars and speeding up the bank's loan closing timelines.
