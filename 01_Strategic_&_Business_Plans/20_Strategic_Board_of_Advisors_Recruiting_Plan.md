### 🏛️ Advisor 1: The Federal Permitting Liaison
*   **Target Profile**: Retired Chief of the Regulatory Branch, **USACE Savannah District** (or retired Senior Mitigation Branch Specialist).
*   **Strategic Purpose**: To navigate the complex Interagency Review Team (IRT) approval pipeline and audit baseline prospectus documents *before* formal submission.
*   **Why Hunter Needs Them**: 
    *   *Regulatory Bypass*: A retired Savannah District Regulatory Chief knows the exact unwritten standards Justin Hammonds and the IRT reviewers look for. 
    *   *Immediate Trust*: Their signature on early site evaluations gives private landowners and investors absolute confidence in the site’s permit feasibility.
*   **The Pitch**: *"Your decades of regulatory stewardship can guide the next generation of ecologically sound stream restorations. Help us build high-gradient banks that exceed USACE mitigation standards."*

### 🎓 Advisor 2: The Fluvial Science & Academic Authority
*   **Target Profile**: **Dr. Laurie Fowler** (or current senior leader at the **University of Georgia Odum School of Ecology / River Basin Center**).
*   **Strategic Purpose**: To provide absolute scientific credibility to the geomorphic and macroinvertebrate modeling, and to establish a direct recruiting pipeline for top-tier UGA interns.
*   **Why Hunter Needs Them**:
    *   *Scientific Gravity*: Having a UGA River Basin Center authority on the advisory board silences environmental activists and EPD bureaucrats who might otherwise challenge stream buffer variances.
    *   *Biological Monitoring*: They can guide the development of the EPT (Mayfly, Stonefly, Caddisfly) biological baseline monitors, proving the ecological lift.
*   **The Pitch**: *"Partner with a firm that is actively applying Natural Channel Design to restore high-gradient wild trout tributaries. Let's create an active outdoor classroom for UGA ecology graduates."*

### 📜 Advisor 3: The Conservation Legal & Land Trust Specialist
*   **Target Profile**: Senior environmental partner at an elite regional law firm (like **Smith, Gambrell & Russell** or **Kilpatrick Townsend**), or Executive Director at the **Georgia-Alabama Land Trust**.
*   **Strategic Purpose**: To govern the drafting of restrictive deed covenants, conservation easements, and clear landowner lien-subordination disputes.
*   **Why Hunter Needs Them**:
    *   *Title Protection*: Securing conservation easements is a legal minefield. If a landowner has an outstanding bank loan, this advisor ensures that banks formally subordinate their liens—protecting the credit inventory from foreclosure risk.
    *   *Tax Efficiency*: They can structure federal and state income tax deductions for landowners donating easements, adding a massive sales incentive for prospective landlords.
*   **The Pitch**: *"Help us protect Georgia's coldwater stream legacies. Your expertise in conservation covenants will ensure that our meander restorations are legally guarded in perpetuity."*

---

### 🏢 Advisor 4: The B2B Credit Demand & Commercial Pipeline Lead
*   **Target Profile**: Senior Land Acquisition Vice President at a national regional homebuilder (e.g., **PulteGroup Georgia**, **Lennar**), or Partner at **Transwestern (Jack Chapman)**.
*   **Strategic Purpose**: To provide an active, direct sales pipeline to liquidate credit inventories as soon as the USACE Savannah District authorizes releases.
*   **Why Hunter Needs Them**:
    *   *Off-Market Liquidation*: Instead of selling credits through retail environmental brokers (who charge 8–12% transactional fees), this advisor connects Blue Ridge directly to corporate VPs who need stream buffer offsets for master-planned logistics parks.
    *   *Market Intelligence*: They provide real-time updates on which sub-watershed basins (HUCs) are experiencing the heaviest residential and commercial permits, directing Hunter where to sit his next JV bank.
*   **The Pitch**: *"Join us in stabilizing credit supply chains in high-growth corridors. Your market insight will guide our next acquisition targets, ensuring you have priority access to premium stream offsets."*

---

### 🚜 Advisor 5: The Heavy Civil & Geomorphic Grading Partner
*   **Target Profile**: Owner or President of a premier local North Georgia civil grading and heavy earthmoving firm (e.g., based in Gilmer or Fannin County).
*   **Strategic Purpose**: To act as the physical construction execution arm—ensuring NCD channel narrowings, log drop steps, and boulder J-hooks are built exactly to Rosgen Level II specs, on-budget and on-schedule.
*   **Why Hunter Needs Them**:
    *   *Fleet Mobilization*: Rather than Blue Ridge buying millions in heavy civil excavators, GPS-equipped loaders, and turbid water pumps, this advisor provides a ready-made grading fleet and field crews.
    *   *Bonding Power*: Heavy stream construction requires significant local performance and payment bonds ($220,000+). Partnering with an established local contractor resolves this constraint.
*   **The Pitch**: *"Let's combine your massive civil grading fleet with my specialized geomorphic design capabilities. Together, we can capture high-margin environmental restorations that standard grading firms cannot build."*
