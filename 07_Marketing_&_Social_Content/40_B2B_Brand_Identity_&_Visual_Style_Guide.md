To help Co-Founder Hunter Morris out-compete multi-million dollar conglomerates, **Save Our Streams Inc.** must maintain an elite, cohesive B2B brand identity. This Visual Style Guide details the corporate color system, typefaces, presentation templates, and logo exclusion specifications to ensure our assets are visually consistent and command a premium credit selling price.

- **River Navy (`#1F4E79`)**: Our primary brand color. Represents institutional trust, deep scoured pools, and professional hydrologic engineering. Use for primary text headers, table headers, and deck backdrops.
- **Trout Teal (`#2C9380`)**: Our core accent color. Represents the clean, oxygenated waters where wild trout thrive and native Rhododendron máximo canopies. Use for highlights, buttons, interactive hover states, and callout box borders.
- **Trout Silver (`#94A3B8`)**: Secondary text color and vector line work. Represents brook trout flank scales and clean mountain sand substrates.
- **Dark Charcoal (`#0F172A`)**: Base body text color. Soft off-black to maximize reading comfort across long technical playbooks.
- **Soft Clay (`#E2E8F0`)**: Divider lines, card borders, and background grid lines.
- **Crystal Background (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft grey-white canvas for all layout backgrounds.

## 3. Typography & Font Pairing Hierarchy

We standardize on modern typography from the **Google Fonts** system to establish a stark visual hierarchy:

### A. Headings: Outfit (Geometric Sans-Serif)
- *Usage*: Page titles, `h1`, `h2`, `h3`, slide titles, and our B2B portal logo.
- *Attributes*: Clean, modern, high-end geometric structure.
- *Styling*: Set headings to **Bold** (`font-weight: 700`) with a tight letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.5px`) to command authority.

### B. Body Copy: Inter (Highly Readable Sans-Serif)
- *Usage*: Paragraph body text, tables, list items, and standard code copy.
- *Attributes*: Clean, highly readable at small font sizes, excellent screen rendering.
- *Styling*: Regular (`font-weight: 400`) or Medium (`font-weight: 500`) with a spacious line-height (`line-height: 1.6`) to prevent reader eye fatigue.

### C. Technical & Math: Georgia (Serif)
- *Usage*: Inline math formulas, geomorphic equations, and financial NPV metrics.
- *Attributes*: Distinct, academic, and highly professional.

## 5. Logo Spacing & Exclusion Zone Standards

Our logo consists of a stylized Brook Trout leaping over a curved geomorphic stream meander, paired with our corporate name.

- **The Emblem**: Must always be rendered in high-resolution vector format (SVG), filled with our primary River Navy or accented with Trout Teal.
- **The Exclusion Zone ($X$)**: To ensure the logo maintains visual clarity and impact, it must be surrounded by a minimum blank space equal to the height of the capital letter "B" in the logo text. Never place border margins or text blocks within this exclusion boundary.
- **Backgrounds**: Only place the full-color logo on white or light-grey backdrops. For River Navy or Dark Charcoal backdrops, use a reversed, all-white version.
