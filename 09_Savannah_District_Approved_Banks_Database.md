# North Georgia Approved Stream Mitigation Banks Database
## Regional Project Registry, Watershed Basin Service Areas, & RIBITS Portal Guide

This document establishes a comprehensive reference database of approved stream mitigation banks operating within the river basins of North Georgia (Coosa, Etowah, Upper Chattahoochee, and Toccoa/Ocoee basins). It provides Hunter with real-world project precedents, bank sponsor contacts, and step-by-step instructions to access the official federal **RIBITS** database for live credit availability and pricing intelligence.

---

## 1. Official Federal Database Portal: USACE RIBITS

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers maintains the official national registry of all approved mitigation banks, in-lieu fee programs, and credit ledgers:

*   **Official Database Link**: [USACE RIBITS Portal (https://ribits.ops.usace.army.mil/)](https://ribits.ops.usace.army.mil/)
*   **Purpose**: Track active banks, credit types (stream vs. wetland), approved service area maps, credit transaction ledgers, and official **Mitigation Banking Instruments (MBIs)**.

### Step-by-Step Guide for Hunter to Navigate RIBITS:
1.  **Access the Portal**: Open the RIBITS home page. On the left-hand navigation panel under **"Filter View"**, select **"Savannah"** from the USACE District dropdown menu. (The Savannah District holds regulatory jurisdiction over all of Georgia).
2.  **View District Banks**: Click on **"Mitigation Banks"** under the district menu. This will display an alphabetical list of all approved, pending, and sold-out commercial banks in Georgia.
3.  **Filter by Watershed/Basin**: To find competitors or projects near Anderson Creek or Noontootla Creek, filter the view using the **"Service Area"** tab. Select your target **Hydrologic Unit Code (HUC)** or river basin (e.g., Coosa HUC 031501, Upper Chattahoochee HUC 031300, Etowah HUC 03150104).
4.  **Audit the Credit Ledger**: Click on any active bank name, and select the **"Ledger"** tab. This shows exactly:
    *   *Total Credits Released* to date.
    *   *Total Credits Sold* (with transaction dates and corresponding USACE permit numbers).
    *   *Remaining Credit Balance* available for sale.
5.  **Examine the MBI Document**: Under the **"Cyber Repository"** tab for the selected bank, download the approved **Mitigation Banking Instrument (MBI)**. This contains the exact geomorphic plans, construction drawings, native planting lists, and monitoring performance standards that the Corps approved for that specific project.

*   **Bank Name**: **Upper Coosa River Mitigation Bank**
    *   *Status*: Approved / Active

## 3. Secondary Reference Database: Georgia LandCAN

While RIBITS is the official USACE regulatory tracking system, the **Georgia Land Conservation Assistance Network (LandCAN)** serves as a vital secondary reference:

*   **Link**: [Georgia LandCAN Mitigation Directory (https://georgialandcan.org/)](https://georgialandcan.org/)
*   **How Hunter Can Use It**:
    *   **Sponsor Directory**: LandCAN maintains an active, public directory of individual bank sponsors, ecological engineering firms, land trusts, and environmental attorneys active in North Georgia.
    *   **Landowner Resources**: Provides specialized guides for private landowners who are interested in conserving their land, establishing restrictive covenants, or setting up a conservation easement to generate tax credits alongside mitigation credits.
    *   **Network Outreach**: Hunter can use the LandCAN sponsor lists to directly identify regional landowners who have already expressed interest in conservation, serving as prime prospects for direct stream restoration pitches.
