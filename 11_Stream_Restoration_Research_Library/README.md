## II. Geographic Coverage & Precedents

To ensure the library provides a truly nationwide and international perspective, the case studies span multiple regions:

### 1. Southeastern United States
* Focuses on high-gradient coldwater trout tributaries, Piedmont agricultural sedimentation, and exurban data center expansions in Georgia, North Carolina, and Virginia.
* Highlights the use of local *Rhododendron maximum* transplanting for thermal protection.

### 2. Northeastern & Midwestern United States
* Features historical industrial-agricultural watersheds with high siltation loads.
* Focuses on Trout Unlimited's pioneering projects in Pennsylvania and the Driftless Area of Wisconsin and Minnesota, where agricultural silt was cleared to expose native spawning gravels.

### 3. Western United States
* Showcases large-scale fluvial geomorphic Natural Channel Design (NCD) and alpine river systems (e.g., California's Upper Truckee River) utilizing Rosgen Priority 1 valley restorations.

### 4. Europe & the Baltic Region
* Showcases Europe's leading-edge dam removal movement under the EU Nature Restoration Law.
* Explores cross-border Baltic restorations (Estonia, Latvia, Finland, Sweden) targeting wild sea trout connectivity.

### 5. Russia & Border Watersheds
* Analyzes the Hiitolanjoki River restoration on the Finland-Russia border, where three historic dams were dismantled to unlock critical spawning grounds for the endangered Ladoga landlocked salmon.
* Highlights sturgeon migratory protection in the Ural and Siberian basins.
