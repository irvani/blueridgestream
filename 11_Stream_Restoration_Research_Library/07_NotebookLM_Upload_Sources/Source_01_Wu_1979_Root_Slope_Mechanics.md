# NotebookLM Source 01: The Wu-Waldron Root Shear Strength Model

* **Full Title**: Strength of Tree Roots and Landslides on Shear Slopes
* **Authors**: Wu, T.H., McKinnell, W.P., and Swanston, D.N.
* **Published In**: *Canadian Geotechnical Journal* (1979)
* **DOI**: [10.1139/t79-025](https://doi.org/10.1139/t79-025)

---

## 1. Executive Scientific Abstract
This seminal publication develops the first mathematical analytical model (the Wu-Waldron Model) to estimate the mechanical contribution of plant and tree root systems to soil shear strength on sloping terrains and riverbanks. Prior geomechanics focused purely on soil-clay matrix shear properties. This paper introduces root reinforcement as an added geotechnical cohesion parameter ($\Delta S$ or $c_r$).

---

## 2. Experimental Methodology
* **Field Site**: Steep, slide-prone forested slopes in Southeast Alaska.
* **In-situ Direct Shear Tests**: Large-scale direct shear boxes ($1.0 \text{ m} \times 1.0 \text{ m}$) were constructed around active, root-permeated glacial till soils to measure shear resistance in place.
* **Tensile Testing**: Laboratory tensile strength tests were executed on thousands of freshly harvested roots of varying tree species (specifically conifers), establishing stress-strain curves and measuring ultimate tensile strength ($t_r$ in $\text{MPa}$) against root diameter.
* **Root Area Ratio (RAR)**: Mapped and logged the count, diameter, and spatial distribution of all roots crossing the potential shear failure planes.

---

## 3. Core Scientific Findings
1. **Root-Soil Shear Reinforcement**: Plant roots crossing a potential shear failure plane behave like flexible, tension-bearing cables, converting shear displacement forces into tensile stresses.
2. **Wu-Waldron Cohesion Equation**: Derived the foundational equation for root-added soil shear strength ($\Delta S$):
   
   $$\Delta S = 1.15 \cdot t_r \cdot \left(\frac{a_r}{a}\right)$$
   
   Where:
   * $\Delta S$ = Added soil shear strength/cohesion (kPa)
   * $t_r$ = Mean tensile strength of the roots ($\text{MPa}$)
   * $\frac{a_r}{a}$ = Root Area Ratio (RAR), cross-sectional root area divided by the soil shear plane area.
   * $1.15$ = A constant accounting for the average root orientation angle relative to the shear plane ($\sin \theta + \cos \theta \tan \phi \approx 1.15$, where $\phi$ is the internal friction angle of the soil).

---

## 4. Applied Relevance to Save Our Streams Inc.
* **Bioengineering Validation**: Provides the mathematical proof required by the USACE Savannah District to justify the replacement of concrete rip-rap with native bioengineered banks.
* **Root Matrix Design**: Explores how dense lateral root networks (such as *Rhododendron maximum*) bind Piedmont clay and sand soils, dramatically increasing bank factor-of-safety values under peak storm shear stresses.
